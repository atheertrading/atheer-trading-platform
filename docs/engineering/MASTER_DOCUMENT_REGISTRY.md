## 1. Registry rules

This registry identifies source documents and preserves their original identifiers and paths. It does not grant approval to documents whose own metadata says proposed, initiated, in progress, or pending. The status column reflects the status stated in the inspected document header, or explicitly notes that the header was not normalized.

The original Library archive remains unchanged and is the source artifact. The registry is a navigation and reconciliation aid, not a replacement for the full source documents.

For every formal document, add:
- Revision and creation/update date.
- Owner/author and decision authority.
- Approval status and evidence.
- Specification status and implementation status separately.
- Supersedes/superseded-by relationships.
- Dependencies and related ADRs.
- Requirements and acceptance-test references.
- Source archive path and content hash where required.

For every Q&A document, capture its original path, topic, related formal specification, requirement/decision references, and whether its conclusion remains current. Resolve conflicts without deleting source history.
